Founding Milestone · Agadir, Morocco

From a 48-Hour Hackathon to the RNSport 2026 Congress

ATLAS was originally built in 48 hours during the RNSport Hackathon, organized as part of the International Congress on Digital Revolution in Sports (RNSport 2026) in collaboration with Code 212 – Université Ibn Zohr (Agadir) and ENSA Agadir.

Selected among more than 60 applicant teams down to 10 finalist teams building AI solutions for major sporting events (from AFCON 2025 to the 2030 FIFA World Cup), our team represented ENSAM Casablanca and subsequently presented ATLAS to researchers and sports performance specialists at the RNSport 2026 International Congress.

Working under strict 48-hour constraints shaped ATLAS's core engineering philosophy: prioritizing end-to-end clarity from player tracking and homography projection to graph-based modeling of team formations, pressing intensity, expected goals (xG) threat zones, and defensive vulnerabilities.

03 // System Architecture

How ATLAS Processes a Match

A modular pipeline separating computer vision extraction, spatio-temporal graph modeling, and interactive web visualization.

Stage 1 // Perception

Detection, Tracking & Homography

Processes raw broadcast footage using YOLO player/ball detection, ByteTrack multi-object tracking, SigLIP team clustering, and pitch keypoint homography to project pixel coordinates onto a standard 105m × 68m pitch plane.

Stage 2 // Graph Intelligence

Equivariant GNN (EGNN)

Constructs frame-level graphs where players act as nodes (`x, y, speed, direction, team, role, pressure`) and spatial interactions form edges, predicting line vulnerabilities, spacing overloads, key-player dependency, and counter-attack risk.

Stage 3 // Decision Interface

Tactical Web Workbench

Built with React, TypeScript, and FastAPI microservices (`auth_service` and `ai_service`), providing coaches with interactive pitch maps, dynamic formations, numerical advantage breakdowns, and zone-level xG heatmaps.
