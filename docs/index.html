02 // ADDED VALUE

Why Equivariant Graphs Over Flat Event Stats

Event logs only record the ball carrier. ATLAS models all 22 players simultaneously with rotation/reflection invariance.

Pitch Symmetry

E(2)-Equivariant By Design

Left-to-right or top-to-bottom attacks share identical graph representations without requiring data augmentation.

Camera-to-Pitch

Single-Camera Homography

Works on standard TV broadcast footage (YOLO + keypoint homography) and native optical tracking feeds (Metrica).

Actionable Output

Predictive 5s Threat Window

Forecasts overload zones and vulnerable defensive lines 5 seconds before a shot or line-breaking pass occurs.
